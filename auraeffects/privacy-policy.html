Privacy Policy — AuraEffects

Effective date: 15 October 2026
App: AuraEffects (com.lucky.auraeffects)
Developer: Batras Industries
Contact: official.batrasindustries@gmail.com

Short version

AuraEffects edits media on your device. This version has no account system, ads, analytics SDK or developer-operated upload service. It does not request Android INTERNET permission. Your imported media and voice recordings are not sent to us by the app.

Media and project data

When you choose a video, photo or audio file using the Android system picker, the app copies that selected file into its private storage so it remains available for editing. Project files store your edits, timing, layer names, keyframes and local media paths. The app does not scan your whole gallery. Files selected from cloud storage may be downloaded by that storage provider before being imported.

Microphone permission

Microphone access is optional and is requested only when you choose voice-over recording. Recording begins after you choose the recording feature and grant permission. It stops when you tap Stop or leave the app; an interrupted recording is discarded. Denying microphone access does not prevent other editing features.

Rendering, exports and temporary files

Preview and export processing run locally. Export may create temporary video, audio and decoded PCM files in the app cache. These temporary export files are normally removed after export or cancellation, but a crash or forced termination can leave cache files until the cache is cleared. You choose where to save exported videos and presets through the Android system picker. If you choose a cloud-backed destination, its provider may upload your export under its own policy.

Storage, retention and deletion

Saved projects, imported copies and kept voice recordings remain in app-private storage until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Deleting a layer does not necessarily delete its imported source copy. This build does not provide a per-project deletion or portable project backup feature. To remove all local projects and copies, use Android Settings > Apps > AuraEffects > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This is permanent. Exported files at your chosen destination remain there until you delete them separately. Automatic Android app-data backup is disabled in the app manifest.

Sharing and external services

We do not sell personal data or transmit your media through this app. Opening the online policy launches your browser and visits GitHub Pages; GitHub and your browser may process connection information such as your IP address according to their own policies. Sending us an email opens your email app; your email provider processes the message. We receive any information you choose to include in that email and use it to respond to your request. Do not include private media unless it is necessary for your support request.

Security and your choices

The app uses Android app-private storage and system file pickers. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected and review the location you choose for exports. You can revoke microphone permission in Android settings. The app remains usable offline for supported local media and editing operations.

Children and policy changes

The app does not ask for age or create child profiles. If you believe you have sent us information that should be removed from a support conversation, contact the address below. Changes to app data practices will require an updated policy; the effective date identifies this version.
