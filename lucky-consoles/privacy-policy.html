How Lucky Consoles handles information when you import, download and play games.

Privacy policy · Effective 11 October 2026 · App package: com.lucky.consoles

Local game libraryOptional online catalogYou control imports

The essentials. Lucky Consoles does not require an account. The current app has no advertising or analytics SDK. Your library, favorites, controls and saves are stored on your device. Online features still contact the services providing catalogs, covers and downloads.

1. Who provides the app

Lucky Consoles is provided by Batras Industries. For privacy questions or requests, contact official.batrasindustries@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the information needed to operate your emulator library:

  • Imported and downloaded game files, file locations, names, console labels, sizes, download progress and catalog details.
  • Favorites, cover-image cache, console preferences, controller layouts, graphics settings, save files and save states supported by each emulator.
  • Local troubleshooting information such as device model, Android version, available memory, emulator exit reasons and detected app stalls. These diagnostics are not automatically uploaded by this app.

When you choose a file with Android's file picker, the app receives access to the files you select. Access may be retained so imports can finish. It does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone.

3. Online catalogs, covers and downloads

An online catalog is optional. When you enable one or download a game, the app connects to that catalog's server, image host and download host. Requests can disclose your IP address, requested URLs, request timing and normal network headers to those providers. A catalog access key may be sent when configured. Those providers may keep service or security logs under their own policies.

Game content is not uploaded merely because you import or play it. Emulator cores and games can have network-dependent features; using such features may communicate with their respective services. Only import catalog files and connect to services you trust.

This policy page is hosted through GitHub Pages when opened online. GitHub may process visitor connection information under GitHub's privacy statement. Reading the bundled copy inside the app does not require fetching this page from GitHub.

4. Support and information you choose to share

The request-game feature opens your email app with the details you entered. You review the recipient and message before sending. If you contact us, we receive the information included in that message, such as your email address, description and attachments. Copying diagnostics places them on your device clipboard; share them only when you choose to.

We use support messages to respond and investigate issues. We do not sell personal information or use the app to serve targeted advertising. Support correspondence may remain in our email records while needed to handle the request and related follow-up; contact us to request deletion.

5. Permissions and background work

Internet and network-state access support online lists and downloads. A foreground data-sync service keeps user-requested downloads and imports running with a visible notification. Android file-picker access is used for selected imports. The app does not require an account sign-in.

6. Retention, deletion and backups

Use Downloads or Settings → Storage and games to remove game files managed by the app. Removing a catalog removes its list, not downloaded games or saves. Deleting an imported copy does not necessarily delete your original file elsewhere on the phone. Save states can be deleted separately where supported.

Local preferences and diagnostics remain until replaced, cleared through Android's app-storage controls or removed on uninstall. Files you exported or kept elsewhere may remain. Android backup and device transfer are enabled and may retain eligible app data according to your device and account settings. Manage those copies through your Android backup settings.

Third-party servers control retention of their own logs. Send us privacy or support-record deletion requests at the contact address above.

7. Security

The app uses Android's app storage and system file picker to handle local data. Online services may have different security practices. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure; avoid sharing sensitive information in support attachments or untrusted catalog files.

8. Children

Lucky Consoles is a general-purpose emulator application and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request its deletion.

9. Changes and contact

We will update the date on this page when the policy changes. An app update may include a revised bundled copy. Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to official.batrasindustries@gmail.com.
