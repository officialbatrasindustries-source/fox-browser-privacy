01

Information We Collect

Fox Browser does not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address or passwords.

Browsing history, bookmarks, downloads and settings are stored only on your device.

02

Incognito Mode

Pages opened in Incognito Mode are not saved in normal browsing history.

Temporary browsing data may be removed when the Incognito session ends.

03

Permissions

  • Internet permission is used to load websites.
  • Storage permission is used to save downloaded files.
  • Microphone permission is used only when you choose Voice Search.
04

Downloads

Files downloaded through Fox Browser are saved on your device.

Users are responsible for downloaded content.

05

Cookies

Websites opened inside Fox Browser may use cookies and local storage.

Those websites manage their own cookies according to their own privacy policies.

06

Third-Party Websites

Fox Browser displays websites from third parties.

We do not control or take responsibility for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

07

Data Sharing

Fox Browser does not sell your personal information.

We do not share your personal information with third parties unless required by law.

08

Security

We take reasonable measures to keep your locally stored information safe.

However, no internet transmission is completely secure.

09

Children's Privacy

Fox Browser is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.
